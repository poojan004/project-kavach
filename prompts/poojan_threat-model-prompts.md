# Poojan Patel - Threat Modeling Prompts

## Prompt 1 - Threat Actor Analysis

Review the available evidence and identify the most likely threat actor responsible for the observed activity.

Provide:

- Threat actor profile
- Possible motivations
- Attack objectives
- Confidence level

---

## Prompt 2 - Attack Path Development

Based on the collected evidence, create a complete attack path.

Include:

- Initial access
- Execution
- Persistence
- Communication
- Impact

Provide reasoning for each stage.

---

## Prompt 3 - Asset Identification

Identify assets involved in the attack scenario.

Classify assets as:

- Internal assets
- External assets
- Critical assets
- Supporting assets

Explain why each asset is important.

---

## Prompt 4 - Threat Assessment

Analyze the observed activity and assess:

- Threat likelihood
- Business impact
- Attack complexity
- Potential consequences

Provide a final risk rating.

---

## Prompt 5 - Mitigation Planning

Recommend security controls that would prevent, detect, or reduce the impact of the identified attack path.

Prioritize recommendations by implementation effort and risk reduction.
